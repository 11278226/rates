7. Your Rights (GDPR Arts. 15–22)

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access (Art. 15)
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16)
  • Right to erasure / "right to be forgotten" (Art. 17)
  • Right to restrict processing (Art. 18)
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20)
  • Right to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21)

You can withdraw or change your advertising consent at any time using the Privacy options button inside the App's Settings. To exercise other rights, email dermostudiosnl@gmail.com. We will respond within one month as required by Art. 12(3).

8. Right to Lodge a Complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) at autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl, or with the supervisory authority in your country of residence.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 16 in the European Union (or under 13 elsewhere). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the App, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The App does not use browser cookies. The Google AdMob SDK uses device identifiers (Advertising ID) that perform a function analogous to cookies for ad serving and frequency capping. You can reset or limit your Advertising ID in your device settings.

11. Data Security

Mortgage scenarios saved within the App are stored using the operating system's standard secure storage (NSUserDefaults on iOS, SharedPreferences on Android). Network requests use HTTPS. Google AdMob applies its own security controls. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
